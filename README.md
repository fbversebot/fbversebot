## What Is FBVerse Bot?

FBVerse Bot is a Windows desktop application for sellers who regularly
manage Facebook Marketplace listings.

Instead of repeatedly entering product details by hand, you can prepare
inventory data, import supported CSV files, configure listing workflows,
and manage recurring tasks from the application.

It is designed for sellers looking for:

- Facebook Marketplace auto posting software
- Bulk listing and CSV inventory workflows
- A Marketplace listing scheduler
- Renew and relist management
- Product, vehicle, and property listing tools

## How the Listing Workflow Works

1. **Prepare your inventory**
   Gather accurate titles, prices, descriptions, images,
   locations, and category details.

2. **Install the appropriate edition**
   Download the Windows installer from the official website.

3. **Activate and configure**
   Enter your license details and follow the setup instructions.

4. **Import or enter listing data**
   Use a supported CSV file or enter information in the application.

5. **Review your listings**
   Check product details, images, pricing, and category selection.

6. **Run your configured workflow**
   Start the listing tasks and monitor progress.

7. **Maintain inventory**
   Review published listings and manage supported renew,
   relist, and inventory actions.

Start with a small test batch so you can confirm that your
data and category settings are correct.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I upload Facebook Marketplace listings from a CSV file?

FBVerse supports CSV inventory workflows. Your spreadsheet
needs to match the fields supported by the selected edition
and listing category. Ask support for the current format.

### Can I schedule Marketplace listing tasks?

FBVerse offers scheduling and queue features. Confirm the
requirements for running scheduled tasks during your demo,
including whether the PC and application must remain running.

### Does it support vehicles and property listings?

The product includes vehicle and property listing workflows.
Available fields and platform options may vary by account,
region, category, and software edition.

### Can I renew or relist existing listings?

FBVerse includes renewal and relisting tools. Available actions
depend on the listing, account, and Facebook's current interface.
Renewal does not guarantee a particular search position.

### Is FBVerse a browser extension?

No. FBVerse is Windows desktop software that works with
supported browser environments.

### Is there a free demo?

A free demo is available through the official support contact.
Ask about the current demo format and what you can test.

### Does one license work on multiple computers?

Licenses are tied to a computer's Hardware ID.
Contact support about transfers or multiple-PC licensing.

### Are proxies included in the subscription?

Proxy support is available, but proxies are supplied separately.
Confirm any additional costs with support.

### Will it guarantee sales or prevent account restrictions?

No. Sales depend on demand, pricing, listing quality, location,
and customer service. Automation cannot guarantee account safety,
listing visibility, leads, or sales.

### Is FBVerse open source?

This profile introduces the FBVerse product and links to official
resources. A public README does not grant access to the application's
source code or change its software license.
